## Tips

- **Bio:** `🦁 እንግሊዝኛ በአማርኛ · 3 ቀን በነፃ · ሙሉ ኮርስ 300 ብር 👇` and the link `t.me/FidelEnglishBot`.

- **How often:** 3–4 times a week. Mix: 1 price post, 1–2 idioms, 1 Reel.
- **Ask a question** at the end of each post, so people comment. Comments make Instagram show your post to more people.
